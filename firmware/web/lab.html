Performance effects

Sliders take effect at once. Hold buttons work while you hold them (mouse, touch, or the keys shown). Everything returns when you let go or set it back to zero.

Impulse Morph

~0 · control rate

Hold a pad to glide the piece somewhere for as long as you hold it; let go and it glides back over about 0.3 s. Pads add together, so four pads give fifteen combinations. Keys 1–4.
