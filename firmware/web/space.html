Glag Space

Sparse percussion and struck voices, with both grain voices feeding on what they leave behind: Grains replays slices of the echo's tape, Haze replays the reverb's tail. Each piece has a tide, a slow shape of quiet, stirring, building, a held breath, the peak, the fall and the ringing, and the sends, the delay and the reverb move with it.
