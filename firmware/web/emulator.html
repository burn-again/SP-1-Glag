The whole control map
Home: F1 tempo, F2 delay, F3 reverb, F4 DJ filter. T1 to T4 open a channel. •• + T changes that channel's voice.
Channel: F1 character, F2 tone, F3 length, F4 register. •• + its own T: extended page (echo, reverb send, pan, activity).
Macro, on a channel or extended page: FFWD tap adds a step (four), RWD held slows its glide (8 s up to 64 s), RWD double tap turns it off.
Harmony: •• + PLAY once. F1 root, F2 mode, F3 drift, F4 motion. •• + T1 to T4 picks the reverb: Hall, Chamber, Shimmer, Bloom; •• + T3 again steps Shimmer's interval (octave, fifth, seventh).
Playworld: hold PLAY 2 s (again, or •• twice, to leave). T1 song mode: on, its pages 2, 3, 4, then off (an outro). T3 the pump: on, page 2, off. T4 a texture: rain, vinyl, tape hiss, surf, off. A bright light: on; a dim pulsing one: the page. The faders follow the last T pressed; what is on stays on when you leave. (T2, arp mode, is planned.)
Anywhere: •• held + faders: the four levels. •• twice: Home. PLAY: grow or rest. •• + PLAY twice: next piece.
Distortion: •• + VOL− steps the amount (off, 1 to 4), •• + VOL+ the type (Chebyshev, overdrive, fuzz, fold, XOR). Held 3 s: VOL− turns it off and resets it, VOL+ moves it before the delay and reverb, after them, or onto the reverb alone. The track LEDs show the type, the play row the amount, filling from the •• end before the effects and from the PLAY end after, blinking on the reverb alone.
Rocker, on Home and Harmony: FFWD tap freezes (tap again to let go), FFWD held freezes until you let go. RWD held slows the record to 10 BPM over 10 s. Anywhere: •• + FFWD randomizes, •• + RWD undoes, •• + RWD held 2 s saves the piece.
