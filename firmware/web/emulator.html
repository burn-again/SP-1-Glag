MIDI in

MIDI channels 1 to 4 play Glag's channels 1 to 4, each in its own voice; while a channel has notes, and for 4 s after, its own music rests. On the Pulse's channel notes are drums, by name in any octave: C kick, D snare, F♯ hat, A♯ open hat, C♯ click. CCs: 1 macro, 7 level, 10 pan, 16 register, 71 character, 72 length, 73 activity, 74 tone, 91 reverb send, 93 echo send. Program change: the piece. MIDI clock: the tempo.

Start the sound first; Chrome or Edge will ask to allow MIDI.

The whole control map
Home: F1 tempo, F2 delay, F3 reverb, F4 DJ filter. T1 to T4 open a channel. •• + T changes that channel's voice.
Channel: F1 character, F2 tone, F3 length, F4 register. •• + its own T: extended page (echo, reverb send, pan, activity).
Macro, on a channel or extended page: FFWD tap adds a step (four), RWD held slows its glide (8 s up to 64 s), RWD double tap turns it off.
Harmony: •• + PLAY once. F1 root, F2 mode, F3 drift, F4 motion. •• + T1 to T4 picks the reverb: Hall, Chamber, Shimmer, Bloom; •• + T3 again steps Shimmer's interval (octave, fifth, seventh).
Playworld: hold PLAY 2 s (again, or •• twice, to leave). T1 song mode: on, its pages 2, 3, 4, then off (an outro). T3 the pump: on, page 2, off. T4 a texture: rain, tape hiss, surf, off. A bright light: on; a dim pulsing one: the page. The faders follow the last T pressed; what is on stays on when you leave. T2 arp mode: every channel but the drums one arp, short; F1 octaves, F2 pattern, F3 division, F4 spread (0: all on the same note).
Album: hold PLAY 10 s and let go (the play row fills to 2 s for Playworld, then again, blinking, to 10 s, where it flashes). The AMB-1 pieces in order, two minutes each, fading one into the next, each in its own key. FFWD skips on, RWD goes back; any other press ends it.
Shuffle: keep holding to 20 s (it fills again, blinking fast, then a light runs round) and let go. Every piece in a random order, no repeats, each in song mode with random settings: by chance arp mode, distortion, the pump (sometimes without drums), a texture, another reverb, reverse, ping-pong, an echo time, the auto filter, macros.
Anywhere: •• held + faders: the four levels. •• twice: Home. PLAY: grow or rest. •• + PLAY twice: next piece.
Distortion: •• + VOL− steps the amount (off, 1 to 4), •• + VOL+ the type (Chebyshev, overdrive, fuzz, fold, XOR). Held 3 s: VOL− turns it off and resets it, VOL+ moves it before the delay and reverb, after them, or onto the reverb alone. The track LEDs show the type, the play row the amount, filling from the •• end before the effects and from the PLAY end after, blinking on the reverb alone.
Rocker, on Home and Harmony: FFWD tap freezes (tap again to let go), FFWD held freezes until you let go. RWD held slows the record to 10 BPM over 10 s; while resting or frozen, RWD taps turn the crank: each plays the next step where something sounds (frozen, the held echo and reverb take that note in, then hold again). Anywhere: •• + FFWD randomizes, •• + RWD undoes, •• + RWD held 2 s saves the piece.
